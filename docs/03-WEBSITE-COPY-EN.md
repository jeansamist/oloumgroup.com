### Buying or renting a prefabricated building: how to choose? `/actualites/choisir-achat-ou-location`
_2026-08-02 · Advice · 6 min_

Period of use, budget, tax, resale: five simple questions to decide whether to buy from Yves Oloume SARL or rent from Bella Location.

The same building can be bought or rented. The right choice depends less on the building than on your project. Here are the five questions we ask every client.

**1. How long will you need it?** Under 18 to 24 months, renting is almost always the better option. Beyond that, buying pays off, especially if the building can serve several projects.

**2. Can you tie up capital?** Renting turns an investment into a predictable monthly cost. Buying creates an asset you can depreciate, resell or reuse.

**3. Will the building need to move?** Both models allow the building to be relocated. When renting, dismantling and return transport are included.

**4. Who handles maintenance?** When renting, maintenance is included. When buying, we offer an annual maintenance contract.

**5. What if you change your mind?** Our long-term rental contracts include a purchase option: part of the rent paid is deducted from the price.

### Racking: 7 criteria for equipping your warehouse properly `/actualites/bien-choisir-son-rayonnage`
_2026-06-18 · Advice · 5 min_

Load, stock rotation, clear height, forklift access: our team's guide to choosing among our 15 racking solutions.

The wrong racking is expensive: wasted space, damaged goods, accidents. Before every project, our technicians check seven criteria.

**Load and format** Light parcels, pallets, long products: each format has its own racking family, from office shelving to cantilever racking.

**Stock rotation** If your products must leave in the order they arrived, live (flow) storage is the best fit.

**Height, floor and circulation** Clear height, slab strength and aisle width determine the number of levels and the type of forklift.

**Standards and safety** Our installations are delivered with load signs, upright protectors and an installation report.

### Site camps: the checklist before you order `/actualites/base-vie-chantier-checklist`
_2026-04-09 · Building sites · 5 min_

Headcount, duration, site access, water and power: everything to prepare so that a site camp is up and running as soon as the teams arrive.

A site camp is planned before the contract is even signed. Here is the list we send to our construction clients.

**Headcount and duration** Number of people housed, number of meals per day, expected duration: these three figures determine the number of modules and the choice between buying and renting.

**Access and ground** Our trucks and our crane must be able to reach the site. Flat, compacted ground is usually enough.

**Power and water** Grid connection, generator or solar: we size the installation to suit the site.

**Regulations** Hygiene, fire safety, sanitary facilities: our site camps meet the requirements of major contractors.
